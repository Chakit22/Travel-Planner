Diagram 02 / Flow

Sequence — "Plan 10 days in Vietnam, July, $4000, 2 people"

Time flows top to bottom. Five lanes: user, browser, API route, Claude, tools/DB. The repeating block in the middle is where the magic compounds — Claude calls a tool, sees the result, calls the next one, until it has enough to write the day-by-day plan.
